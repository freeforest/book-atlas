# Community Code of Conduct

Book Atlas contributors are expected to make participation respectful,
welcoming, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Respect differing experience, language, identity, and viewpoints.
- Critique ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Protect privacy: do not ask for or publish personal libraries, notes, paths,
  credentials, account details, or other sensitive material.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and moderation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, doxxing,
deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information are
not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close participation, or ban a
participant when necessary to protect the community. Security vulnerabilities
must follow `SECURITY.md`; do not disclose sensitive details in a public issue.

A private conduct-reporting channel has not yet been configured. Establishing
one without publishing a maintainer's personal address is a release gate in
`docs/RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md`. Until then, do not include private or identifying
material in a report.
